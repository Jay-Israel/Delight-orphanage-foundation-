About Delight Orphanage Foundation

A Christian foundation devoted to children and families — rooted in faith, guided by compassion.

Who We Are

A community of care, built on faith

Delight Orphanage Foundation is a Christian based non-profit organization that is dedicated to supporting children and families through child sponsorship, food and grocery assistance, educational support, mentorship, community outreach and Christian encouragement with the support of our donors, volunteers and partners.

DOF aims to grow and spread to different parts of the earth establishing love, care and also giving hope to the less privileged.

Our Mission

DOF is called out to bring light to the nations and to spread and preach hope to the hopeles. We want to raise up a standard by any possible means to establish love and ultimately preach Jesus to them

Our Vision

To look after the welfare of orphan children by providing them with the necessities of life, educational opportunities, creating health and wellness support. We focus on making the lives of orphans and abandoned children better in all aspects..

What Guides Us

Our Values

The principles that shape every program, partnership and decision we make.

Faith

We root our work in Christian faith, trusting God's provision and guidance in all we do.

Compassion

We approach every child and family with genuine empathy and care.

Dignity

Every person we serve is treated with respect and worth.

Integrity

We commit to honesty, transparency and accountability in our work.

Service

We serve with humility, putting the needs of children and families first.

Hope

We believe every child's story can carry hope for a brighter tomorrow.
