About Delight Orphanage Foundation

A Christian foundation devoted to children and families — rooted in faith, guided by compassion.

Who We Are

A community of care, built on faith

Delight Orphanage Foundation is a Christian organization dedicated to supporting children and families through child sponsorship, food and grocery assistance, educational support, mentorship, community outreach and Christian encouragement.

We exist to stand in the gap for children and families facing hardship, offering not only practical help but also hope, dignity and a sense of belonging.

Our Mission

To serve children and families through compassionate care, education, mentorship and Christian encouragement — helping them build brighter, more hopeful futures.

Our Vision

A community where every child is cared for, every family is supported, and the love of Christ is shared with the world.

What Guides Us

Our Values

The principles that shape every program, partnership and decision we make.

Faith

We root our work in Christian faith, trusting God's provision and guidance in all we do.

Compassion

We approach every child and family with genuine empathy and care.

Dignity

Every person we serve is treated with respect and worth.

Integrity

We commit to honesty, transparency and accountability in our work.

Service

We serve with humility, putting the needs of children and families first.

Hope

We believe every child's story can carry hope for a brighter tomorrow.
