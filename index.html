Who We Are

Rooted in faith. Driven by compassion.

Delight Orphanage Foundation is a Christian organization committed to walking alongside children and families in need — offering practical support, education, mentorship and the hope found in Christian encouragement.

We believe every child deserves care, dignity and the opportunity to thrive, and every family deserves a community that stands with them.
